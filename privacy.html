Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Privacy by design.
Your weekly check-in choices, saved moves, and completion history in the current web experience are stored locally on your device. Monday Money Move LLC does not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The current web app stores your weekly selections, saved money moves, and completion history in local browser storage so your progress can appear when you return on that device.

Information you may provide to us

If you contact support, purchase a membership, or otherwise communicate with Monday Money Move LLC, we may receive information such as your name, email address, transaction or membership reference information, and the content of your request.

Payments

Website payments are processed by Stripe. Purchases made through an Apple app, if offered, are processed by Apple. Monday Money Move LLC does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

How information may be used

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the service
  • Respond to support, privacy, and membership requests
  • Process and administer memberships
  • Protect the service against fraud, abuse, or misuse
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations

Your choices

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing site data or browser storage for Monday Money Move on your device. You may also request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you directly provided to us by emailing vered@mondaymoneymove.com.

Children

Monday Money Move is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security and third parties

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the current service. Third-party providers such as payment processors may process information under their own privacy terms.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The effective date above identifies the current version.
