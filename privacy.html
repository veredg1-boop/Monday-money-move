Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Monday Money Move LLC respects your privacy. The weekly choices, saved money moves, completion history, and reminder preferences in the current app are stored locally on your device. We do not sell personal information.

Information you provide

If you contact support, join a membership, or otherwise communicate with us, we may receive information such as your email address and the content of your request.

Payments

Website payments are processed by Stripe. App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Monday Money Move LLC does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

How information may be used

  • Provide and improve the service
  • Respond to support requests
  • Manage memberships and prevent abuse
  • Meet legal and accounting obligations

Your choices

You may delete locally stored progress by removing the app. You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you directly provided to us through the support contact published with the app.

Children

Monday Money Move is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The effective date above will identify the current version.
