## First five content ideas
1. **One Money Move Monday** — a simple weekly action under $25.
2. **Debt Move of the Week** — one small step toward a high-interest balance.
3. **Savings Move of the Week** — one automatic transfer idea.
4. **Spending Reset** — one expense to review, pause, or cancel.
5. **Investing Habit** — one consistency-focused action without market-timing language.

## Visual direction
- Deep forest green
- Soft mint
- Warm cream
- White
- Rounded cards and buttons
- Calm, minimal, trustworthy look
- Strong "M" mark with subtle upward movement/arrow
- Avoid stock-market cliché imagery, luxury imagery, cash piles, or aggressive sales styling

## Public positioning
**One smarter move each week.**

Primary promise: Help users identify one clear financial action to take now.

Tone: calm, encouraging, practical, nonjudgmental, simple.

## Important launch rule
Do not promise individualized financial, investment, tax, credit, accounting, or legal advice. Keep all public copy educational and general.
