SUPPORT

How can we help?

Monday Money Move is designed to feel simple, clear, and easy to use. If something isn’t working as expected, contact us and include a short description of what happened and the device you’re using.

Contact Monday Money Move LLC

Email: vered@mondaymoneymove.com

For app, membership, privacy, or account questions, email is the best way to reach us.

Membership help

Website memberships are processed through Stripe. Purchases made through an Apple app, if offered, are managed through the customer’s Apple Account subscription settings.

Privacy requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you directly provided to Monday Money Move LLC by emailing vered@mondaymoneymove.com.

Using your weekly check-in

Your saved moves and completion history in the current web experience are stored locally on your device. If you clear browser site data, that locally stored history may be removed.
