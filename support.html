Support

We want Monday Money Move to feel simple, clear, and useful.

Before contacting support

Close and reopen the app, confirm that your device is connected to the internet, and make sure you are using the latest version.

Membership help

App Store subscriptions can be viewed or canceled in your Apple Account subscription settings. Website memberships are managed through the Stripe customer communication provided after purchase.

Privacy requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided to Monday Money Move LLC. A company-domain support email will be added before public App Store release.
